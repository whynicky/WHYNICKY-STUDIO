A slim curved seam runs from the outer shoulder into the sleeve. Solid matte natural cotton, sculptural boxy torso. Zip completely closed. Photograph a single complete garment from straight in front in convincing invisible-mannequin style, softly three-dimensional, arranged neutrally and symmetrically except for the garment's actual asymmetric construction. No words, logos, labels with text, borders, collage, duplicate garments, or watermarks.

Clothing: stone grey cropped boxy cotton jacket with zipped tall standing funnel collar and angled welt pockets, extreme wide washed black jeans and matte black boots. Photo only, no text, no logo, no typography, no border.
